# 💻 VARSITY MENTORHUB

# 🚀 About the Project

Varsity MentorHub is a student mentorship platform designed to connect university students who need academic guidance with tutors and mentors who can support their learning. The project combines tutor discovery, profiles, booking, messaging, learning resources, and accessible user journeys in one collaborative platform.

### Project Vision

To make academic mentorship more accessible and help students find guidance that supports their learning and academic development.

### Project Objectives

- Connect students with suitable tutors and mentors.
- Support knowledge sharing and collaborative learning.
- Provide clear tutor discovery, profile, booking, and communication journeys.
- Build an accessible platform around student and tutor needs.
- Deliver the product through organised Agile teamwork and continuous improvement.

### Project Approach

Our team used Agile practices and Trello to manage the product backlog, sprint work, responsibilities, progress, blockers, and reviews. The project evidence includes user stories, task breakdowns, product and sprint backlogs, and individual Agile logbooks.

# 👨🏽‍💻 My Role and Contribution

I served as the **Analyst** for Varsity MentorHub. My contribution focused on research, requirements analysis, user-flow review, data and privacy considerations, project risk communication, and business-partnership planning. I also supported design reviews, sprint progress updates, presentations, and retrospectives.

### Research and Requirements Analysis

- Helped define core screen-navigation requirements and user-flow scope.
- Reviewed and approved low-fidelity concepts for the home screen.
- Coordinated a design-review meeting and collected team feedback on UI mock-ups.
- Signed off the final UI mock-up set and updated sprint progress on Trello.

### Data and Privacy Analysis

- Designed the visual symbols and layout structure for the entity-relationship diagram.
- Researched data-privacy requirements for user credentials and system logs.
- Verified dataset mapping against the Tier 1 and Tier 2 subscription models.
- Finalised the database schema design and signed off the ERD model.

### Business and Partnership Analysis

- Established partnership-program objectives and the high-school outreach scope.
- Structured pricing tiers and community-group rates for high schools.
- Drafted the high-school partnership engagement workflow and agreement terms.
- Reviewed the partner strategy and contributed to approval of the subscription-tier structure.

### Presentation and Team Contribution

- Reviewed presentation content for clarity and consistency.
- Presented project risks, mitigation measures, and the MVP validation approach.
- Participated in retrospectives and identified improvements to slide design, delivery, and validation strategy.
- Supported backlog revision and planning for subsequent sprints.

### What I Learned

Varsity MentorHub strengthened my ability to move between user needs, interface decisions, data structures, privacy considerations, and business requirements. I learned that analysis is most useful when it turns research and feedback into decisions that the team can act on and verify.
